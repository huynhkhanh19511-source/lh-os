## Legend

- **Fit Hypothesis**: High / Medium / Low
- **Outcome EV**: Expected value of getting the role (High / Medium / Low)
- **Info EV**: Expected information gain about market or self (High / Medium / Low)
- **Decision**: Apply / Watch / Reject
- **Status**: Queued / Applied / Interviewing / Rejected / Offer / Withdrawn
