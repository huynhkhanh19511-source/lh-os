# Contract Analysis Artifact — Sample Agreement

## Executive Decision View

| Area | Signal | Decision Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Provider has explicit monthly delivery obligation | Track delivery acceptance criteria |
| Payment | USD 2,000/month, due within 15 days of invoice | Manage invoice timing and cash collection |
| Confidentiality | Continues for 2 years after termination | Maintain post-contract information controls |
| Termination | Requires material breach + 30-day cure period | Immediate exit is not generally available |
| Liability | Capped at 3 months of fees | Quantifiable downside boundary |

## Structured Summary

### Rights
- Either party may terminate for uncured material breach.

### Obligations
- Provider must deliver monthly analytics outputs.
- Client must pay monthly fees.
- Both parties must preserve confidentiality.

### Constraints
- Payment is due within 15 days after invoice receipt.
- Breach must remain uncured for 30 days before termination.
- Provider liability is capped.

## Primary Decision

Before entering the agreement, clarify delivery acceptance criteria because the payment obligation is explicit while acceptance criteria are not specified in the sample text.

## Confidence
Medium — derived directly from explicit clauses; no interpretation of external law was performed.
