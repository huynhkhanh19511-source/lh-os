# Sample Contract — Service Agreement

## Parties
- Client: Alpha Retail Co.
- Provider: Beta Analytics Ltd.

## Scope
Provider will deliver a monthly sales analytics report and dashboard.

## Term
The agreement begins on 2026-09-01 and continues for 12 months.

## Fees
Client will pay USD 2,000 per month within 15 days of invoice receipt.

## Confidentiality
Both parties must keep non-public business information confidential during the agreement and for 2 years after termination.

## Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice.

## Liability
Provider's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid during the 3 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
