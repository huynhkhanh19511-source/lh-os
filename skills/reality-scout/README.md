# Reality Scout

> Turn unstructured Reality into structured, inspectable signals.

Reality Scout is a core skill in LH OS for observing and structuring Reality before interpretation, decision, or intervention.

It does not attempt to solve Reality.

Its purpose is to make Reality more visible.

---

## Why

Reality rarely arrives in a clean format.

It may appear as:

- a job description
- a customer conversation
- a market signal
- a repository
- a system log
- a metric
- an event
- feedback

Raw information is not automatically understanding.

Before a system can reason about Reality, Reality must first become observable.

```text
Reality
    ↓
Contact / Evidence
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Reality Scout
    ↓
Structured Representation
```

---

## Boundary

Reality Scout is responsible for:

- observing
- extracting signals
- mapping context
- identifying possible patterns
- exposing constraints
- preserving unknowns

Reality Scout is not responsible for:

- making the final decision
- recommending an intervention
- designing the solution
- forcing a conclusion

```text
Reality Scout
      ↓
Structured Representation
      ↓
Gap Analysis / Decision / Other Skills
```

---

## Core Principle

> Observation is not interpretation.

Reality Scout keeps these layers separate:

```text
Observation
    ↓
Interpretation
    ↓
Hypothesis
```

Example:

**Observed**

> The job description requires production deployment within 60 days.

**Interpretation**

> Speed-to-production appears to be an important constraint.

**Hypothesis**

> The organization may value deployment-oriented architecture over purely advisory architecture.

**Unknown**

> The actual internal deployment process is not yet known.

---

## Workflow

### 1. Capture

Collect raw Reality.

Possible inputs:

- documents
- conversations
- repositories
- logs
- metrics
- market events

### 2. Extract Signals

Identify observations that may be relevant.

### 3. Map Context

Ask:

- Who is involved?
- What is happening?
- What constraints exist?
- What relationships matter?
- What changed?

### 4. Detect Emerging Patterns

Connect multiple signals into possible patterns.

Patterns remain hypotheses until supported by further evidence.

### 5. Produce a Reality Map

Structure what is currently known, inferred, and unknown.

## Output

The primary output is a **Reality Map**.

```markdown
# Reality Scout Report

## Scope

What part of Reality is being observed?

## Observations

Directly observable information.

## Signals

Relevant observations.

## Context

Where do these signals exist?

## Emerging Patterns

Possible patterns suggested by multiple signals.

## Constraints

What limits the system?

## Unknowns

What is still missing?

## Hypotheses

What requires validation?

## Confidence

How confident are we in the current model?

## Next Question

What should be investigated next?
```

## Evidence Model

Reality Scout distinguishes between:

```text
Observed
    ↓
Interpreted
    ↓
Hypothesized
    ↓
Validated / Rejected
```

A useful Reality Map should never hide uncertainty.

Unknowns are part of the output.

---

## Failure Modes

Reality Scout may fail when:

- interpretation is mistaken for observation
- patterns are inferred from insufficient signals
- confirmation bias filters contradictory evidence
- unknowns are hidden to force a clean narrative
- context is ignored
- AI-generated assumptions are accepted as Reality
